Every channel changes how a message lands

A communication channel is the medium a message travels through: email, chat, a call, a meeting.

1 · Efficiency

Instant, searchable, global. Tashkent and London work in real time.

2 · Overload

More messages than people can process. Quantity rises, quality falls.

3 · Distance

Text carries no tone or face. Neutral sounds cold; quick sounds careless.

What worked, and where the limits are

✔ What worked

Fewer low-value messages.

Right tool for the right task, chosen by employees.

Managers trained first: culture change is led from the top.

! The limits

Email not switched off: still used for customers, partners and legally binding messages.

No evidence the policy raised profit.

Context matters: a small team with daily face-to-face contact may not need it.

What should managers do?

Three recommendations: disconnect to reconnect

1 · Set channel norms

Agree what goes where. Reduces overload.

Urgent→ call
Routine updates→ chat
Formal decisions→ email

2 · Protect real conversation

Feedback, conflict and bad news go by voice or face to face.

3 · Train and review

Managers model the norms. Check every quarter that they still fit.

The risk of ignoring this: teams physically together, but emotionally absent, like the café.
